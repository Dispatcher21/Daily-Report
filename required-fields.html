Mark Required / Hidden

Click any box on the report below to require or hide that field. A required field blocks Generate Report until it's filled in; a hidden field is removed from the on-screen form entirely (and can't stay required).

Field Order

Drag a card (or use its arrows) to change where it appears on the report-entry form. This is laid out the same way the real form is -- it doesn't affect the printed PDF, which always keeps its fixed layout.
