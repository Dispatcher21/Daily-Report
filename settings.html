Security▸

Saved Passwords

The company password and every custom setup's password, kept in a form only someone who knows the admin password can read back. Confirmed fresh every time, never remembered on this device.

Change Passwords

Admin Password

Changes who can unlock admin access on a device -- doesn't affect anyone already unlocked, and doesn't affect the company password. You'll be asked to confirm the current admin password to make this change.

Company Password

Every other device stays on the old password until someone manually opens Settings → Leave This Company, then rejoins with the new one. There's no way to push the change to them automatically -- tell your team before (or right after) you do this. Nothing already synced is lost either way; a device just won't sync anything new until it rejoins.
