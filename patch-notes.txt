DAILY WORK REPORTS -- PATCH NOTES
==================================

Newest first. Dates are when the work shipped.

0.0712 -- 2026-10-08
  - Home page redesign. Uses the whole window: for managers, the
    Manager Dashboard on the left and your projects on the right. On
    phones, tabs switch between Projects and Dashboard.
  - Dashboards are made of widgets. Tap Edit Dashboard to add, remove,
    resize or reorder them, or make your own (pick what to count, the
    date range and how to split it). Your layout follows your account to
    other devices. The project page's dashboard works the same way.
  - Inspectors see what needs them at the top of the home page: reports
    sent back for changes or unlocked, and this week's reports.
  - New Report button at the top of the home page: pick the project and
    start writing.
  - Folders without dragging: each project card has a menu (Put in a
    folder, Take out, favorites, move earlier or later), and Organize lets you tap several
    projects and put them in a folder at once. A long press on a phone
    starts Organize. Folders open in place, with a back link. Dragging
    with a mouse still works.
  - Project page redesign: a header with the project's status, day of
    the contract, days left and percent complete; the Workspace (new
    report, reports, quantities, Pay Apps, recent reports, project info)
    next to the dashboard.
  - Progress Over Time now runs from NTP to the completion date with a
    dashed on-pace line, so ahead or behind is easy to see. Pay App
    stars are kept.
  - Manager page is now a review inbox: everything waiting on you, with
    a preview to approve, send back with a note, or comment without
    leaving the page. The next item opens on its own. Reports with
    nothing flagged can be approved together.
  - Approved reports and Pay Apps are locked. Nobody can change them
    until a Manager or admin unlocks them (with a reason). The author can
    ask for an unlock from the report or from My Reviews. Edits after an
    unlock send it back for approval, marked Resubmitted.
  - Inspectors get a My Reviews page (in the menu): what was sent back,
    what's waiting, and what was approved recently.
  - Fixed: reports an inspector wrote opened read-only for them, even
    with "Edit/delete their own reports" on.
  - Project cards with a photo keep their name readable in light mode.

0.0612 -- 2026-10-08
  - Reports hold up to 10 photos (was 6).
  - New Summary and Photos page, printed only when it's needed. When the
    Work Summary is too long for page 1, it continues on page 2, with the
    top half for the rest of the summary and the bottom half for 4
    photos. With 4 photos or fewer, this page takes the Photo Log's
    place, so the report stays 2 pages; with more, the Photo Log follows
    as page 3. Page 1 says "(Continued on page 2)", and long Pay Items,
    Tests and Checks tables split by row under the same headings.
  - With more than 6 photos, the extra ones print on the Summary and
    Photos page after the Photo Log. Photos keep their numbers from the
    editor wherever they print.
  - Printed Work Summary starts right under "Short Work Summary": the
    empty row and the lines between them are gone, giving the summary
    more room.
  - Saving a report makes fewer requests to the photo storage.

0.0601 -- 2026-10-08
